# Mission: cart-crud

> **Purpose:** an optional exploration of the cart as data: add, read, remove, and whether it survives navigation. No verdict.
> **Used when:** passed to the agent after the charter (`--mission cart-crud`), once `core` works.

**Theme:** the cart as data: create (add), read (badge, button, list), delete (remove), and whether it survives navigation.
**Expectations to check:** D1–D4 (and C1, C5 on every step).
**Verdict:** none. Findings are classified by reproducibility.

## Steps

1. Log in with the given account.
2. Add the **Backpack** on the products page. Record the badge, the button label, and the cart contents. Remove it again on the products page and record the same three things. (D1)
3. Add the **Backpack** and the **Bike Light**. Open the cart and remove the **Bike Light** there. Go back to the products page and record the badge and both button labels. (D1)
4. Try to add the Backpack a second time. Record what is possible. Then add **all six products**, record the badge, and remove them all. Record the badge and the cart contents. (D2)
5. Add the Backpack and reload the page. Record badge, button and cart. Use the browser **Back** button once and record the same. (D3)
6. Log out and log in again. Record whether the Backpack is still in the cart and whether the badge matches. (D3)
7. Open the cart, start checkout, then click **Cancel**. Record the cart contents. (D4)

## Free exploration

Budget: 12 actions. Ideas, not a checklist. Choose your own:
- Add and remove items in a different order, or from a product detail page instead of the list.
- Use the Back button and a reload at different moments (cart open, mid-checkout).
- Compare the badge, the button labels and the cart list after every change.
- Anything else that creates, reads or deletes cart contents.
